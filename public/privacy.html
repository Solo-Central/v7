privacy policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

overview

Solo Central is a web proxy service. We are committed to being transparent about what data we do and do not collect. This policy explains our practices clearly.

data we do not collect

We do not collect, log, store, or sell:

  • Your browsing history or the sites you visit through the proxy
  • Personal information such as your name, email, or IP address
  • Cookies or session data from proxied sites
  • Any form of usage analytics tied to your identity

how the proxy works

Traffic routed through Solo Central passes through our servers solely to facilitate access. We do not inspect, log, or retain the content of your requests. Proxied traffic is not associated with any user account or identifier.

third-party services

Solo Central uses the following third-party services which may have their own privacy practices:

  • particles.js — loaded from jsDelivr CDN for the particles background
  • Google Fonts — loaded for typography (Inter font)
  • open-meteo.com — used on the home page for weather data if you grant location permission; your coordinates are sent directly to their API and not stored by us

geolocation

The home page may request your browser's geolocation to display local weather. This permission is optional, requested by the browser, and your location is never sent to our servers — it goes directly to the open-meteo weather API.

cookies

Solo Central does not use cookies for tracking. Any cookies set during a proxied session belong to the destination site and are handled within the proxy context.

changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
